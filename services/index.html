How to Pick the Right Service

Start with the source. If it is a pipe, go to the pipes and plumbing group. If it came from a storm, see weather and building envelope. Floors and walls pages help once the water is out and you are weighing repair options.

Many jobs involve more than one page. A burst pipe may need extraction, structural drying and drywall repair. The crew handles these as one plan.

Emergency situations are marked in the page title. If water is moving, do not wait. Call first and read second.

Each service page follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, so categories, drying goals and monitoring mean the same thing from one page to the next.

What Every Job Includes

  • Safety steps and source control.
  • Water extraction.
  • Moisture mapping with meters and thermal imaging.
  • Drying with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  • Daily readings and a drying log.
  • A written scope for repair and insurance.

Scope and price are explained before work starts. We also record photos and readings that help you with any claim you choose to file.

Emergency or Scheduled? How to Tell

Call right away when water is moving, a ceiling is sagging, a basement is taking on water or sewage is present. These are emergencies because the damage grows with every hour.

A stained wall, a musty smell or a soft floor can wait a day, but not a week. Hidden moisture keeps spreading, and mold can begin within a few days.
