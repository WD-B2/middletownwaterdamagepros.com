River Valley Conditions and What They Mean for You

The Great Miami River shapes life in Franklin. After the 1913 floods, the Miami Conservancy District built dams upstream to protect valley towns. Those dams lower river flood risk, but local drainage and groundwater still matter.

Look up your address on the FEMA flood map service and ask your insurer what your policy says about flood. Standard homeowners coverage often excludes it, so many river area owners add a separate policy.
