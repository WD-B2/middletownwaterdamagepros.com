If Water Is Still Spreading in Trenton

Stop the source

Close the main valve or the appliance shutoff if it is safe to reach. For a heater, close the cold supply on top of the tank.

Cut power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.

Protect and photograph

Lift valuables and papers onto dry surfaces. Photograph the water and damage for your insurance claim.
